## Full description

```
Your PDFs are private. Keep them that way.

PDF Toolbox works 100% on your device. Your contracts, invoices, IDs and
scans are never uploaded anywhere — there is no server to upload to.

7 TOOLS, ZERO SIGN-UP

• Merge PDFs — combine files in any order, losslessly
• Extract pages — see page thumbnails, tap the ones to keep (or type 1-3,5)
• Images to PDF — turn photos & screenshots into a single PDF
• Compress PDF — shrink files up to 90% for email (2.3 MB → 175 KB in our test)
• Watermark — stamp DRAFT / 机密 / CONFIDENTIAL on every page, any language
• Protect PDF — add a password so only you can open the file
• Transfer to PC — send files to your computer over WiFi. No cable, no cloud

WHY OFFLINE MATTERS

"Free" PDF sites make money by uploading your documents to their servers.
PDF Toolbox has no server. Everything happens on your phone, and files only
go where YOU send them — email, chat, or your own cloud drive.

NO ADS. NO ACCOUNTS. NO TRACKING.

Free forever. About 7 MB.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission abuse — network is used only for the WiFi
  phone-to-PC transfer on your own local network
• No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no data collection of any kind

Support & feedback: use the Issues tab in this repository.
```
