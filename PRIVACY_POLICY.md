# Privacy Policy — PDF Toolbox

Last updated: 2026-09-06

## Short version

PDF Toolbox does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. There is
no server, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no account system.

## How the app handles your files

- All PDF processing (merge, extract, compress, watermark, protect, images-to-PDF)
  happens entirely on your device.
- Your files are only read from and written to the locations you choose via the
  Android file picker.
- The app never uploads your documents to any server operated by the developer.

## Network permission

PDF Toolbox requests network access for exactly one feature: **Transfer to PC**.
When you start the transfer server, the app listens on your **local WiFi network
only**, so a computer on the same network can exchange files with the phone.
Files transferred this way go directly between your devices and never touch the
internet or any third party. The server is protected by a random key and only
runs while you keep it turned on.

## Permissions explained

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | Local WiFi transfer to your own computer only |
| Access network state | Detect the local network address to show the transfer URL |

## Data we collect

None. The developer cannot see what you do in the app.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this repository with an updated
date above.
